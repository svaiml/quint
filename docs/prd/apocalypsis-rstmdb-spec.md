### Architectural Principle: Computational Icon

The state machine is an ICON in the Orthodox theological sense: a window into divine reality, not a simulation of it. An icon depicts what is real but does not claim to capture the fullness of the reality it depicts. Likewise, this state machine maps observable indicators to revealed states without claiming to capture the fullness of the eschatological plan.

**Palamite Foundation**: St. Gregory Palamas distinguished between divine ESSENCE (ousia -- unknowable, inaccessible to created beings) and divine ENERGIES (energeiai -- the real manifestations of God in the created order that ARE accessible). The state machine models ENERGIES: observable manifestations in history, ecclesial life, moral conditions, geopolitical events. It does NOT model ESSENCE: the hidden divine plan, the timing of events, or the interior life of the Trinity.

- **Patristic debate**: Which historical entity is "Babylon"? Rome (early Fathers), secular civilization (later Fathers), any totalizing anti-God system (modern Orthodox reading).

#### S7: Battle of Armageddon
- **Revelation chapters**: 19:11-21
- **Andrew of Caesarea**: Christ's victory over the assembled forces of evil. Not a human military battle but the divine conquest of evil by the Word of God.
- **Observable indicators**: Decisive confrontation between the Church and organized anti-Christian forces. Outcome: evil defeated, beast and false prophet destroyed.
- **APOPHATIC BOUNDARY**: The mechanism of Christ's victory exceeds created modeling capacity. The state machine can identify indicators of approach but cannot model the divine action itself.

#### S8: Binding of Satan / Millennium
- **Revelation chapter**: 20:1-6
- **Orthodox reading (majority, following Augustine and Andrew)**: AMILLENNIAL -- the millennium is the Church age. Satan "bound" at Christ's first coming (Incarnation, Cross, Resurrection). The "first resurrection" is baptism. The saints "reign with Christ" through the life of the Church.
- **Alternative Orthodox reading (minority, following Irenaeus)**: A future period of Christ's earthly reign. This is a theologoumena (permissible opinion), not dogma.
- **CRITICAL**: The Orthodox tradition does NOT endorse dispensationalist premillennialism (Darby/Scofield). The minority Orthodox premillennial view differs fundamentally from dispensationalism in that it does not include a rapture and does not systematize history into dispensational periods.

#### S9: Final Rebellion / Gog and Magog
- **Revelation chapter**: 20:7-10
- **Andrew of Caesarea**: After the symbolic millennium, Satan gains release for a short time. Gog and Magog represent the final anti-God coalition, not identifiable with specific modern nations. The rebellion is universal in scope but brief.
- **Observable indicators**: Worldwide rebellion against God after a period of relative ecclesial stability. The MONITOR verb tracks whether current conditions match this pattern.

#### S10: Last Judgment + New Heaven and New Earth
- **Revelation chapters**: 20:11-22:5
- **Great White Throne Judgment**: Universal, inescapable, righteous. Every person judged by their works and their relationship to Christ.
- **New Heaven and New Earth**: Total cosmic renewal (not destruction). "Behold, I make all things new" (Rev 21:5). The New Jerusalem descending as a bride.
- **ABSOLUTE APOPHATIC BOUNDARY**: The nature of the new creation exceeds all created modeling capacity. The state machine terminates here. "Eye has not seen, nor ear heard, nor have entered into the heart of man the things which God has prepared for those who love Him" (1 Corinthians 2:9).
