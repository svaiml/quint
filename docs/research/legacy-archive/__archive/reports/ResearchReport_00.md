## Critical Assessment

### Strengths
- Comprehensive landscape scan — useful as a reference index
- Clear tier structure separates signal from noise
- The 7-layer model is a solid mental framework for the pipeline
- Reports 01-02 correctly identify stable anchors (AllBeads, Goose, Aider, LangGraph)
- Report 04 architecture deep-dives are genuinely insightful

### Weaknesses / Red Flags
1. **Confirmation bias** — Reports 03-04 increasingly reflect what was asked about rather than objective rankings
2. **Sycophantic framing** — Report 03 opens with "You were right" and elevates discussed people to DOMINANT
3. **Hallucination risk** — Some entries hard to verify (Shadow Beads, Ghost Worktrees, AgeMem)
4. **No citations or links** — Pure assertion, no way to verify rankings
5. **Milestone numbering error** in Report 01 — "Milestone 2" appears twice
6. **Duplicate entry** in Report 02 Layer 7 — SGLang listed twice (#12 and #13)
7. **Claims vs content mismatch** — Report 03 claims 175 items but shows ~15

### Pattern: Gemini's Prompt-Sensitivity
- Reports 01-02: Relatively neutral landscape scan
- Reports 03-04: Whoever was discussed in the chat got "DOMINANT" status
