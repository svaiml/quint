## Priority Study Plan

### Month 1: Foundations
| Book | Author | Why | Gap It Fills |
|---|---|---|---|
| "The Mystical Theology of the Eastern Church" | Vladimir Lossky (1944) | THE definitive work on apophatic theology as epistemological method. Grounds Tier 0. | Formal apophatic theology vocabulary |
| "The Essential Peirce Vol. 2" | Charles Sanders Peirce | Mature work on abduction, pragmaticism, classification of sciences. Full system beyond just abduction. | Using only 20% of Peirce. Semiotics, existential graphs, categories all map to Infer. |

### Month 2: Formalization
| Book | Author | Why | Gap It Fills |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Conceptual Mathematics" | Lawvere & Schanuel | Gentlest category theory intro for structural thinkers. Your tier/mapping thinking IS category theory without the formalism. | Formalizing structural thinking for publication |
| "Logic for Computer Science" | Jean Gallier | Connects formal proofs to computation. Then Curry-Howard correspondence (proofs = programs). | Infer engine IS a proof system - need formal vocabulary |

### Month 3: Depth
| Book | Author | Why | Gap It Fills |
|---|---|---|---|
| "On Difficulties in the Church Fathers: The Ambigua Vol. 1-2" | Maximus the Confessor (Constas translation, Harvard 2014) | Definitive critical edition. Logoi doctrine = Tier -2 foundation. | Primary source depth for Maximus |
| "The Book of Why" | Judea Pearl & Dana Mackenzie (2018) | Connects do-calculus to everyday reasoning. Readable. Grounds causation in product. | Causation formalization for Infer |

### Month 4: Publishing
| Resource | Type | Why | Gap It Fills |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 papers from "Theology and Science" journal | Journal articles | Understand the format, style, and what gets accepted at the science-theology intersection | Academic publishing methodology |
| "Writing Your Journal Article in 12 Weeks" | Wendy Belcher | Practical methodology for turning insights into peer-reviewed papers | How to publish to establish IP priority |
